Shorts Reply Bot

A small personal tool that helps one YouTube creator reply to comments on their own Shorts.

What it does

  • Reads new comments on the owner's recent Shorts.
  • Drafts a short reply to each one.
  • Posts the reply from the owner's own channel.

It skips spam and never posts links. It only works on the channel that signed in to it, and nobody else can sign in or use it.
